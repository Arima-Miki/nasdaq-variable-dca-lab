## 4. Research question

### 4.1 The `O-4` question

> **Can the C-1 methodology chain across the authorized research span be established with sufficient
> documentary continuity to evaluate `HG-8`?**

### 4.2 The proposition `HG-8` requires

Carried unchanged from `…sequencing_decision.md` §7.1:

> For the intended span, the candidate's governing methodology chain is reconstructable: **every
> methodology change effective within that span is identified and dated from authoritative

### 4.3 The eight determinations required, per candidate

| # | Determination |
| - | ------------- |

| **2** | Whether **methodology changes occurred** within the bounded span |
| **3** | The **effective date** of each identified change |
| **4** | Whether the **sequence of methodology versions can be ordered** |
| **5** | Whether **continuity between successive versions** can be established |

## 8. Evidence hierarchy

`O4-OD-07`. Fixed **before** research begins, and consistent with the PRIMARY / NEAR-PRIMARY /
SECONDARY / UNREAD tiering already used in this study.

| Tier | Class | Examples of what qualifies |
| ---- | ----- | -------------------------- |
| **Tier 1** | **Dated official methodology documents and official methodology-change notices**, publisher-issued | A methodology document carrying an in-body effective or version date; a publisher-issued change notice, rule-change announcement, or index-alert stating a change and its effective date |
| **Tier 2** | **Official publisher pages or archives establishing version identity, effective date, or continuity** | A publisher-operated version register; a publisher-operated document library listing versions; a publisher page stating that a document supersedes another |
| **Tier 3** | **Other primary publisher material that can corroborate the chain** | Publisher consultations and proposals; publisher notices that bear on a change without dating it; publisher material establishing document identity |

### 8.1 The primacy rule

> **Secondary sources MUST NOT silently substitute for missing primary methodology evidence.**

A gap in Tier 1 evidence is recorded as a **gap**. It is not filled by a secondary description of
what the primary document is said to contain. **`SC-7`** applies: secondary material is never
promoted.

### 8.2 `G-OD-08` — authoritative non-publisher evidence

`G-OD-08` is unchanged and controlling: **"dated and documented" does not require a publisher-side
decision document.** Primary documentary evidence from an **authoritative issuer or other
authoritative party** may establish that a change occurred and its effective date.

Such evidence is classified **Tier 1 for the fact it establishes**, and must be recorded as
issuer-side rather than publisher-side. **It does not close a publisher-side provenance gap**, and
the gap must not be erased or downgraded — the standing treatment of `N-3`.

### 8.3 Conditions for consulting secondary evidence — defined in advance

`O4-OD-08`. Secondary material **may be consulted** only where **all** of the following hold:

1. Tier 1–3 primary avenues for the specific fact have been exhausted under the §9 protocol;
2. it is used **only** to locate or identify primary material — as a **pointer**, never as the fact;
3. it is recorded and classified **SECONDARY**, and the fact it points to remains **UNESTABLISHED**
   until primary material is obtained;
4. it is **never** used to establish a methodology change, an effective date, a version ordering, or
   continuity;
5. its use is disclosed in the evidence artifact, with the gap it failed to close stated explicitly.

> **A conclusion resting on secondary material alone is not a resolution of `O-4`.**

### 8.4 Archived copies

A third-party archive of a publisher-issued document is **publisher content obtained via a
non-publisher intermediary**. It is classified **Tier 2 at best**, and must carry the caveat that
the archived copy's fidelity to the publisher original **could not be verified against the
publisher**. It may **not** be promoted to Tier 1 by virtue of its content.

## 9. Search protocol

`O4-OD-09`. Designed **before** its results are observed, per `AC-1`.

### 9.1 Access boundary — binding on every step

Every retrieval must be made with **no** account, login, credentials, entitlement request, payment,
subscription, trial, personal-information disclosure, publisher contact, terms acceptance, or
access-control bypass. **D-9 remains in force: entitlement and registration work is NOT AUTHORIZED.**

Also prohibited: rate-limit evasion; scraping around a blocked interface; undocumented or private
endpoints discovered through circumvention; automated access where no readable access policy permits
it (**`SC-14`**); retrieval of historical observations; retrieval of date spines beyond what is
already held; series construction; and any empirical calculation.

**Copying credentials, cookies, session tokens, or personal information into any store is
prohibited.**

### 9.2 Domains and repositories

| Class | Scope |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Publisher document repositories** | The Nasdaq-operated index document repositories from which the Stage-C methodology set was obtained |
| **Publisher index-version register** | The publisher-operated register of index versions, names, symbols, return versions, currencies and base dates |
| **Publisher change / notice channels** | Publisher-operated methodology-change notices, index alerts, rule-change announcements, and consultation outcomes |
| **Publisher document libraries** | Publisher-operated listings that enumerate document versions or supersession |
| **Archives** | Third-party archives of the above, **Tier 2 at best**, per §8.4 |

**Not in scope:** distributor sites; data-vendor sites; entitlement-gated channels; any source
requiring an account.

### 9.3 Document classes sought

Index methodology documents governing the index; the calculation manual governing return types and
currency treatment; the index-version register; the general index methodology guide; the
recalculation policy; and any **superseded version** of any of these, together with any publisher
notice dating a transition between versions.

### 9.4 Version / effective-date strategy

1. Establish, for each currently held document, its **version identity and in-body date** where one
   exists — the open `O-5` limitation is that at least one currently held document carries no in-body
   effective date.
2. Seek **prior versions** of each document class.
3. Seek **publisher statements of supersession** — the strongest continuity evidence, because it
   links two versions directly.
4. Where a version is located without a date, record it under §9.9.

### 9.5 Change-notice strategy

Search publisher change/notice channels across the bounded span for notices that state a change and
its effective date. The known `N-3` publisher-side gap is a **specific target**: a publisher-side
document dating that change would close it. **Its absence must not be re-recorded as closure.**

### 9.6 Archive strategy

Archives are consulted **only** to locate publisher-issued material not otherwise reachable, subject
to §8.4 tiering and to §9.1's automated-access constraint.

### 9.7 Candidate-specific terms

Per §5, searches must be run **per candidate**: the official name, the symbol, and the return-version
designation for each of `NDXJPY`, `XNDXJPY`, `XNDXNNRJPY`, in addition to index-level terms. A hit on
an index-level document does **not** discharge the per-candidate determination unless the document's
own scope statement covers that version.

### 9.8 Stopping conditions

The protocol stops when **any** of the following holds:

1. every class in §9.2–9.7 has been searched per candidate and the §4.3 determinations are answered;
2. the determinations cannot be advanced further within the access boundary, and the residue is
   recorded as a gap;
3. a §15 stop or escalation condition triggers;
4. continuing would require an action §9.1 prohibits.

> **Exhaustion of the protocol is not evidence that no further material exists.** It is a statement
> about the search performed.

### 9.9 Treatment rules — fixed in advance

| Situation | Required treatment |
| --------- | ------------------ |
| **Inaccessible document** | Record as **ACCESS-LIMITED** with the access barrier named. **Not** an absence of the document, and **not** `SC-5` |
| **Known to exist but not retrievable** | Record its existence, the evidence for its existence, and the barrier. The fact it would establish remains **UNESTABLISHED** |
| **Undated material** | Record as **UNDATED**. It may establish that a document version exists; it may **not** establish an effective date. If a change is known to have occurred but cannot be dated, **`SC-4` triggers** |
| **Superseded material** | Retain and record. Supersession is continuity evidence, and a superseded version is often the strongest Tier-1 material available |
| **Contradictory publisher evidence** | Record **both**, without reconciling them by preference. Do **not** silently prefer the more recent, the more convenient, or the more favourable. Escalate under §15 |
| **Incidental observation values** | Per **D-5** and S.2's minimisation rule: retain the minimum, **do not analyse**, record that values were incidentally returned. `SC-16` triggers only if a determination would require **analysing** values |

## 13. Anti-circularity controls

### 13.1 The eight prohibitions, binding on the research

The research **must not**:

1. **change the `P1-5` principle** after seeing `O-4` results;
2. **move the `O-4` research floor**;
3. **move the K1 cutoff** after seeing evidence;
4. **shorten the span** because evidence is difficult to recover;
5. **select methodology versions because they improve performance**;
6. **use empirical performance** to determine documentary admissibility;
7. **convert D-10 into a new hard gate**;
8. **treat `H-1` as established** merely because methodology continuity is established.

`OD-P15-12` already forbids items 1–3 and, in substance, item 4. Any of these appearing necessary is
a **stop-and-escalate** condition under §15 — **never** an adjustment made inside the research.

> **On item 8 specifically:** methodology continuity and live status are **different propositions**.
> Establishing that a documented methodology governed a period says nothing about whether values for
> that period were calculated and disseminated in real time. `H-1` remains **NOT ESTABLISHED**, and
> `SC6-OD-05`'s distinction — *"not established as LIVE" does not mean "established as NON-LIVE"* —
> remains mandatory.

> **On item 7:** D-10 remains the study-level OD-12 evidentiary standard. It is not a gate, adds no
> gate, and `HG-8` remains the sole remaining qualification-blocking gate. Its residual conditions
